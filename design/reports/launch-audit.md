# Pre-launch audit — report

Run 2026-10-04 from [`launch-audit-brief.md`](launch-audit-brief.md) (D145): 31 Sonnet auditors on a frozen
build (`bbcdc8b`), two local copies of the production catalogue and production read-only, each scope then
re-checked by a separate verifier; plus the coordinator's read-only look at the production admin. Evidence
for every row is in the run's scratchpad, `out/<SCOPE>.md` (not committed).

The completeness round's six gap checks were cut short once (owner: *"make things quicker and finish in
10 mins"*) and then re-run time-boxed (owner: *"finish the 6 checks in 10 min"*); their results are in
*Gap checks* below and were not put through a separate verifier. **The frozen
build is behind `main`**: D139b (photo loader) and D142 (part number, M+S columns) landed after it, so a data
finding about part numbers or M+S must be re-checked on `main` before it is fixed.

## Verdict

No P0. **The storefront can launch once the P1 items below are done**; five of them are owner actions
outside the code (password reset, Converge production account, Vercel plan, legal terms, the quote-email
flag). The rest are code fixes that can be applied in this session after approval.
